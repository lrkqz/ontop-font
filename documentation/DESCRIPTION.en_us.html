On Top is a wide, heavy display typeface drawn from the ON TOP logo of @lrkqz. The logo holds four letters, O, N, T and P; every other glyph was designed in their logic, from measurements taken off the logo, and drawn as vectors in code. No outline comes from another font.

The letters behave like a monoline geometric face stretched 1.32 times in width. Every rounding is the corner of the logo's O, straight letters and stroke ends are square, and the letters with three storeys (B, E, S, 2, 3, 5, 8 and their kin) carry three equal bars, so that their counters stay open at small sizes. On Top sets in capitals: the lower case is mapped to them. Stylistic sets offer a round A (ss01), an R with a long leg (ss02), an S with square ends (ss03) and a square E (ss04).

To contribute, see github.com/lrkqz/ontop-font.
