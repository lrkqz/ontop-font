FONTLOG for On Top
==================

This file provides detailed information on the On Top font software. This information should be
distributed along with the On Top fonts and any derivative works.


Basic Font Information
----------------------

On Top is a wide, heavy display typeface drawn from the ON TOP logo of @lrkqz. The logo holds
four letters, O, N, T and P; every other glyph was designed in their logic, from measurements
taken off the logo (docs/MEASUREMENTS.md), and drawn as vectors in code (sources/glyphs.py). No
outline comes from another font.

One style, Regular. Capitals only: the lower case is mapped to the capitals. Character set: GF
Latin Core. Stylistic sets: ss01 round A, ss02 R with a long leg, ss03 S with square ends, ss04
square E.

The sources are Python: sources/geometry.py (the drawing primitives), sources/glyphs.py (every
glyph and the rules it follows), sources/spacing.py (sidebearings and kerning). python build.py
writes the UFO sources/OnTop-Regular.ufo and builds the fonts from it with gftools builder
(sources/config.yaml).
